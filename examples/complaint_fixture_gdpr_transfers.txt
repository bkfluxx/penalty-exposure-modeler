COMPLAINT (excerpt, style of a DPA-framed cross-border transfers complaint)

1. The complainant is a user of the respondent's consumer messaging service,
which is offered in the Union and processed by the respondent's Irish
establishment.

2. The respondent transferred personal data of approximately 533 million users
to servers in the United States for the purpose of operating its advertising
business. Those transfers continued after the Court of Justice of the European
Union annulled the adequacy decision on which the respondent had relied.

3. The respondent invoked standard contractual clauses as its transfer tool but
did not adopt supplementary measures. The complainant submits that the
respondent thereby infringed Article 46(1) GDPR, and that the infringement is
subject to administrative fines under Art. 83(5) GDPR because the provisions
breached fall within Article 44 to 49.

4. Separately, the respondent failed to maintain appropriate technical and
organisational measures to ensure a level of security appropriate to the risk,
contrary to Article 32 GDPR. An internal audit identified misconfigured storage
buckets affecting 4.2 million accounts, and the respondent did not notify the
supervisory authority of that breach within the period required by Article 33
GDPR. The complainant submits that these infringements fall within Art. 83(4)
GDPR.

5. The respondent was previously the subject of an infringement finding by the
supervisory authority in 2021, and the processing described above continued for
at least 38 months after the complainant first raised it with the respondent.

6. The complainant asks the supervisory authority to impose an administrative
fine, and notes that the supervisory authority has previously fined the
respondent EUR 225 million for transparency infringements and EUR 405 million
for processing children's data.
